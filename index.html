HUMAN CONTROL

Autonomous execution.
Intentional decisions.

The system should remove repetitive production work, not critical judgment. Creative assets are reviewed before distribution. Paid campaigns are prepared inside AA Console and pushed to Meta Ads Manager for human management.

  • Final creative approval stays with your team
  • Paid media management stays in Meta Ads Manager
  • Authentic proof is preserved and verified
  • Attribution closes the feedback loop
  • Exceptions are flagged instead of silently published
